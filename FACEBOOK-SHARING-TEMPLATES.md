## 🎨 Design Tips for Facebook Posts

1. **Use emojis liberally** – They break up text and increase engagement
2. **Keep paragraphs SHORT** – 2-3 lines max before a line break
3. **Bold key phrases** – Use **asterisks** for emphasis
4. **Include links** – Always point to repositories or resources
5. **Add hashtags** – 5-8 relevant hashtags at the end
6. **Use questions** – Hook people with "Is another world possible?"
7. **Create urgency** – "Join us now," "The time is now," "We're building this"
