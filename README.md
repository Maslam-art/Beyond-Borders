# Beyond-Borders
# Beyond Borders and Beyond Currency

A poetic collection of ghazals and nazms  
that moves beyond walls,  
beyond wealth,  
and beyond the silence that follows injustice.

It speaks for the wounded,  
the poor,  
the displaced,  
and the forgotten.  
It listens to the grief of mothers,  
the hunger of children,  
and the ache of a world  
where money often speaks louder than mercy.

This collection is not only verse.  
It is a witness.  
It is a question.  
It is a prayer.  

Through sharp reflections and tender sorrow,  
it looks at greed, war, inequality, hypocrisy,  
and the fragile dignity of human life.  
It asks whether humanity can still rise  
above borders,  
above currency,  
above fear.

## Themes

- Peace and compassion.
- Poverty and inequality.
- Justice and resistance.
- Humanity beyond religion, nation, and class.
- Critique of greed, power, and violence.
- Hope, dignity, and moral awakening.

## About the Collection

These poems are written in a voice that is intimate, urgent, and socially aware.  
They carry the tradition of ghazal while speaking to the present moment.  
Their language is rooted in pain, but their purpose is to preserve conscience.  

The collection reflects on the cost of division and the price of indifference.  
It remembers that a border can divide land, but never fully contain the human heart.  
It remembers that currency can measure exchange, but never the worth of a soul.

## Inspiration

The title **Beyond Borders and Beyond Currency** reflects a belief that  
love is larger than division,  
and humanity is greater than profit.  
It imagines a world where peace is not a dream,  
but a shared responsibility.

## Format

- Poetry collection
- Ghazals and nazms
- Social and spiritual reflection
- Humanitarian themes

## Message

This work stands for a world where the suffering of one is seen by all,  
where compassion crosses every boundary,  
and where the value of life cannot be reduced to money.
