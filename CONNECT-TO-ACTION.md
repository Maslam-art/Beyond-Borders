## 📋 Our Framework: Abundant Earth Constitution

These poems are the **heart** of a larger movement. The **mind** is taking shape in:

### **Open-Source-Draftv2.0**
A currency-free, open-source global constitution inspired by:
- **Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam** (The world is one family)
- **Resource-Based Economy** principles
- **Sustainable Development Goals** (SDGs)

**Key Components:**
- **Article 1:** Resource Declaration – AI global inventory of Earth's resources
- **Article 2:** Sustainability Trust – Protection for future generations
- **Article 3:** Community Governance – Open-source democratic participation
- **AI Resource Allocation:** Intelligent distribution of abundance
- **Pilot Project:** Sandhikharka Summit 2026

**Explore the Framework:**
👉 [Open-Source-Draftv2.0 Repository](https://github.com/Maslam-art/Open-Source-Draftv2.0)
